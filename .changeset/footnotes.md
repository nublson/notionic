Footnotes: links to blocks under a "Footnotes" heading render as numbered superscripts, and the section renders as a footnote list with back-links. Configure the heading names, show a title, or replace the list with the `footnotes` option and `components.footnotes`. `collectHeadings` leaves the footnotes heading out, and `blocksToMarkdown` writes GFM footnotes.
